Where the dose went

Why your ears keep a budget

Hearing damage is dose, not drama: it adds up across every loud thing in the day, and once hair cells are gone they do not grow back. NIOSH sets the budget at 85 dB for 8 hours, with the 3 dB exchange rate - every 3 dB louder halves the safe time. That makes a 100 dB motorcycle a 15-minute-a-day sound and a 110 dB concert a 1.9-minute one. Headphones at full volume sit near 95-105 dB at the eardrum, which is why the loudest hour of most people's day is the one they chose.
